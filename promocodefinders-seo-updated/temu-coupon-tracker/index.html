How to interpret the tracker

  • Checked by publisher means the code was manually reviewed on the date shown.
  • Offer to check describes the promotion that may be presented to an eligible account; the exact value and terms can differ.
  • Region is deliberately conservative because Temu campaigns can be account- and location-specific.
  • The retailer's in-app terms and final checkout total are authoritative for your account.

Why the date does not simply change every day

The verification date only changes after a real check is recorded. Automatically printing today’s date without a check would make the tracker look fresher than the underlying evidence. PromoCodeFinder uses a single verification data source so the same recorded date can appear consistently across tracker pages.
