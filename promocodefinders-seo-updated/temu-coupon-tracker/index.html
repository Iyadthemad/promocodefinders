How to interpret future entries

Read our verification methodology. A future test entry should record the actual test date, account audience, delivery region, observed terms, restrictions and evidence. A result for one account does not establish universal availability. An unsuccessful test must be recorded in its context rather than used to declare a code expired everywhere.
