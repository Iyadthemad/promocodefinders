Coupon vs sale price: which deal actually costs less?

Compare the amount you will pay for the items you need, including delivery and fees. A bigger percentage discount can still leave you with a more expensive basket.

By PromoCodeFinder · Updated October 30, 2026. Examples below are hypothetical, not retailer offers.

The quick comparison

Final cost = basket price − discount + shipping + taxes and mandatory fees. Use the checkout total when taxes depend on the delivery address. Compare the same items, quantities, sizes and delivery options. Do not count a future coupon bundle as money saved on today's order.

Worked example: 20% off can lose to a sale

You need one item. One shop offers a coupon on a $50 price; another has the same item on sale. Assume no additional taxes or fees for this simplified example.

The sale costs $3 less. The coupon's percentage is larger than the sale's apparent saving, but delivery changes the result. If the sale has different return costs or arrives too late, consider those tradeoffs before choosing.

Should you add an item for free shipping?

Suppose your basket is $42, delivery is $6 and free shipping starts at $50. Adding an $8 item takes the total from $48 to $50: you spend $2 more today. That may make sense if the extra item was already on your shopping list at a fair price. If you do not need it, paying delivery is cheaper.

Check whether the shipping threshold applies before or after discounts. A coupon can push a qualifying basket below the threshold. Recheck the delivery line after applying it.

Percentage off or a fixed discount?

For the same eligible basket, compare the actual amounts. Fifteen percent of $60 is $9, so a $10 coupon saves $1 more before considering shipping or other differences. A percentage coupon may have a maximum discount or apply only to selected items. Use the eligible subtotal rather than the entire basket.

Two sequential discounts also do not simply add together. If a retailer allows 20% off $100 and then another 10% off the reduced price, the total is $72, a 28% reduction. Whether any offers combine is determined by that retailer's terms and checkout.

Five checks before ordering

  1. Eligibility: check region, account restrictions, minimum spend and excluded products.
  2. Accepted discount: confirm the deduction is visible before paying.
  3. Whole basket: count any extra purchase needed to unlock the offer.
  4. Cashback: check compatibility, tracking conditions and payout uncertainty. Treat it separately from checkout savings.
  5. Future rewards: check expiry dates and required future spending. A bundle's face value is not the same as cash you can use now.

What if the coupon does not work?

Read the error message and offer terms before trying another code. For Temu, see our coupon troubleshooting guide and new vs existing user guide. Our coupon tracker records publisher check dates; account-specific eligibility still needs confirmation with the retailer.
