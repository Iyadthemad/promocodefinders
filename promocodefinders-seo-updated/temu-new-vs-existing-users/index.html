Temu codes: new vs existing users

An offer aimed at new users is not automatically available to every shopper. Check the specific definition and conditions Temu displays rather than relying on a label on a coupon site.

Do not equate a new installation with eligibility

Installing an app again does not establish that an account meets a promotion's definition of a new user. Read the offer before proceeding. This site cannot determine account eligibility or override Temu's decision, and creating additional accounts is not a reliable way to qualify.

What should existing users check?

Begin with the offers actually visible to the account you use. Compare their restrictions with your planned basket. A returning shopper may see a different result from someone else, so a screenshot or a discount mentioned by another person does not prove your eligibility.

Which of our codes is for new users?

We do not have current campaign terms confirming a new-user or gift offer for ALI015848. We also cannot confirm whether ALE832781 or ALI240834 includes existing users. The code comparison page preserves all three codes without assigning unsupported eligibility.

Read gift and bundle offers carefully

If an offer mentions a gift, check the actions required to qualify and any purchase or delivery conditions. If it lists a coupon bundle, inspect each coupon's minimum spend and expiry. Several coupons intended for separate orders are different from a single discount on today's order.

If the account does not qualify

Keep your original budget and compare the purchase without the promotion. If the item is no longer worthwhile, there is no need to buy it simply because you started a campaign. If the displayed terms appear to include your account but redemption fails, use the troubleshooting guide.
