About PromoCodeFinder

PromoCodeFinder is an independent shopping-information website focused on clearer promo-code pages, useful eligibility notes and product-find guides.

What we do

Our goal is to organize promotional information so shoppers can quickly understand what a code is, what it may offer, how it can be checked and what conditions could affect eligibility.

How we approach offers

Promotions can change quickly. We avoid presenting a promotional result as guaranteed when the retailer may vary it by account, country, order, campaign or other conditions. Important pages include a review date so readers can judge freshness.

How PromoCodeFinder is funded

Some links may be affiliate links. If a visitor makes a qualifying purchase through one of those links, PromoCodeFinder may earn a commission at no additional cost to the visitor.

See our Affiliate Disclosure for more information.
