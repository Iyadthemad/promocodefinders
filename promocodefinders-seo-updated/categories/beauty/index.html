Beauty and hair care: compare the whole basket

Compare the exact product and pack size before treating two beauty listings as alternatives. This is a shopping checklist, not a product-performance test or a claim that a product suits your skin or hair.

Match the product before comparing price

Check the full product name, edition, volume or weight, and pack quantity. A listing image can show a set while the selected option contains a single item. Compare the manufacturer or retailer ingredient list and usage directions for the exact edition you plan to order.

Single product or set?

What to compare for each purchase type
PurchaseCompareReason to skip
Sheet-mask multipackNumber of masks and the assortment in the selected pack.Several included variants are unwanted.
Hair-care setEach item and size, compared with buying only what you need.The set duplicates products you already have.
Cleanser or serumExact edition, volume, packaging and product directions.The listing does not clearly identify the selected product.

Use unit prices carefully

For comparable liquids, divide price by volume and multiply by 100 to compare price per 100 mL. For example, a hypothetical 50 mL bottle at 15 currency units costs 30 per 100 mL. A 100 mL bottle at 24 costs 24 per 100 mL. These are arithmetic examples, not live prices or evidence of product quality.

Do not compare a mixed set using total volume alone: its items do different jobs. Work out whether you want each item and whether a single product meets the same need.

Check the delivered total

  1. Select your destination and confirm whether the basket can be delivered.
  2. Review shipping charges and any taxes or other charges disclosed during checkout.
  3. Check the retailer's return information for the items you chose.
  4. If you try a code, compare the final total before and after applying it.
