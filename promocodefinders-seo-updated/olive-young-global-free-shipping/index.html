Currency, taxes and payments

According to Olive Young's billing policy, products are displayed in US dollars. Your issuing bank may apply a different conversion rate. The retailer accepts international cards and eligible international PayPal accounts, subject to payment availability. The shipping guide separately warns that local VAT, goods tax, customs duties or courier collection fees may apply.

What happens below the minimum?

If your basket is below the applicable threshold, shipping may be charged. Do not add unnecessary products solely to cross a promotional threshold: compare the extra product price with the actual delivery fee. Confirm the amount after any code is applied, because the checkout determines the qualifying total.

Returns and order restrictions

The official information says returned items generally must be shipped back within two months for a refund. Review product eligibility, return-postage responsibility, customs forms and customer support instructions before ordering; free outbound shipping is not the same as free returns.

Frequently asked questions

Is Olive Young Global shipping free over $60?

Its official shipping guide lists free shipping from US$60 for multiple destinations, including the UK, Canada, Australia and Singapore. Exceptions and current checkout conditions apply.

Does a coupon count toward the shipping threshold?

The checkout calculation determines whether your basket qualifies; do not assume the pre-discount total qualifies after a promotion is applied.

Are customs fees always included in the free-shipping offer?

No. Shipping and import taxes are separate. The official policy notes that customs duties or courier fees can apply, depending on the destination.

Can international orders be refunded?

The official shipping information says refunds generally require shipment back within two months, but product and order-specific return terms should be checked.
