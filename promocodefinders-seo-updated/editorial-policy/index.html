How we describe codes and shopping guides

A published code is a code to check, not proof of a discount. We separate editorial updates from evidence that an offer was successfully redeemed.

What the current status means

The codes currently listed on this site—ALE832781, ALI240834, ALI015848 and USEME123—are retained from our existing listings. We have no documented successful redemption or current merchant terms establishing their discount amount, expiry or eligibility. They are labeled not independently verified.

What would support a stronger claim?

A claim about a discount needs supporting merchant terms or a documented checkout result, including when it was checked, the region, relevant account restrictions and basket conditions. One successful checkout would describe that situation; it would not prove that a code works for everyone.

When evidence is missing, we do not invent a discount percentage, expiry date, test count, success rate or customer rating. A failed attempt alone also does not establish that a code is expired for all shoppers.

Dates and changes

An editorial review date describes a content review. It is not a coupon test date. We do not automatically advance review dates each month or use a countdown without documented campaign terms.

Shopping guides and product links

Our guides provide measurement, comparison and checkout checklists. Product descriptions and retailer links are starting points for comparison, not claims of hands-on testing, current stock or proven performance. When we cannot confirm a supplied coupon price, it is not displayed as a current price. Promotional feature claims are turned into checks for the selected option rather than treated as tested specifications. Confirm the exact listing details with the retailer.

Affiliate relationships

Some retailer links may earn us a commission from qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. Copying a code does not automatically activate an offer. Read the affiliate disclosure and check the final payable amount before ordering.

Report a correction

See our contact page. Include the page URL, code, country, date and the error or terms you observed. Do not send passwords, payment details or unredacted order information.
