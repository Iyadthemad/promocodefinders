Temu discount codes UK: GBP checkout guide

Shopping with a UK delivery address? Check a code against the basket total in pounds, not a dollar-denominated promotional headline.

Last checked: 28 October 2026. This is the publisher's code check date, not confirmation of savings or a live delivery quote.

Important: Codes, discounts, shipping estimates, fees, returns and payment options depend on the delivery address, account, product, seller and campaign. The Temu checkout and current policy pages take precedence over this guide.

UK currency, taxes and postcode checks

UK shoppers should compare amounts in pounds sterling (GBP). An advertised $100 voucher bundle should not be treated as £100 off: coupon denominations, exchange presentation and thresholds must be read as shown in the UK app. Check VAT and all charges at checkout for your actual delivery address, rather than converting a US screenshot.

Shipping time and fee: the checkout is the source

We cannot substantiate one guaranteed number of delivery days or one universal shipping fee for this country. Temu may show different estimates for local-warehouse items, international sellers and individual delivery addresses. Before paying, record the estimate on each item and the final order shipping charge. Do not treat a promotional shipping banner as proof every seller and basket qualifies.

Returns and refund conditions

Temu frequently advertises returns for many eligible products within a window of up to 90 days, but exclusions exist and some electronics have shorter policies. Inspect the product return badge, the order return window, packaging requirements and any charge for an additional return from the same order. Do not assume 90 days is universal.

UK payment options

Temu's regional payment help page lists major cards, PayPal and digital wallets among several options; availability can vary by account and checkout. Choose only a method shown on your payment screen.

How to redeem a Temu code by region

  1. Set your real delivery country and ZIP or postal code on the Temu account you will use.
  2. Search the code in the Temu app if it is an app-search campaign; otherwise use the stated checkout coupon field.
  3. Confirm the offer applies to your country, account and selected items.
  4. Read the discount cap, eligible products and minimum spend, then compare shipping, taxes and final total.
  5. Only order when the final payable price is acceptable without relying on unearned future vouchers.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a guaranteed UK Temu discount code?

No. Search the code on your UK delivery account and read the campaign terms. Savings vary by account, region and time.

Does Temu always show a fixed delivery fee in the UK?

No single fixed delivery charge or delivery time has been verified for every order. Check the shipping total and estimated delivery date for the selected items and postcode.

Can I use a welcome coupon if I have ordered before?

A campaign may limit eligibility based on account and order history. Reinstalling is not proof of first-time eligibility.
