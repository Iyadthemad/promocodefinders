How to use Temu promo codes

Start with a purchase you already want to make. Then check whether an offer reduces the total for that purchase, using the account and delivery destination you plan to use.

1. Copy the complete code

Use a copy button on our Temu coupon page or select the visible code manually. Avoid leading or trailing spaces. A copy confirmation means only that text reached your clipboard; it does not mean the promotion was activated.

2. Read the offer's entry instructions

After opening Temu, sign in if needed and confirm your shopping region. Follow the code-entry route shown on the promotion itself. Do not assume a code belongs in a checkout field when the campaign specifies a different route. If there are no instructions and no offer appears, do not treat that as a successful redemption.

3. Inspect any coupon or campaign that appears

Look for account restrictions, eligible products, minimum qualifying spend, expiration and maximum savings. If several coupons appear, write down the conditions for the one relevant to your planned order. Adding the face values of several coupons can overstate the saving available on a single basket.

4. Check your exact basket

Select the right size, quantity, material and product variant. A displayed price for another variant is not a useful comparison. Review any shipping charges and other amounts shown in the order summary, and check whether selecting one promotion removes another.

5. Confirm the payable total before payment

Look for the applied discount in the final summary. If it is missing, pause and investigate before ordering. Keep a record of the terms and total for your own reference if you proceed. Never assume that entering a code guarantees a later adjustment.

A quick example

Suppose you planned a $25 basket and a coupon requires $40 of eligible items for $6 off. Increasing the basket to $40 leaves $34 to pay before any other charges—$9 more than originally planned. A larger headline saving may still mean spending more.

Need a different kind of help?

Use the account eligibility comparison if you are unsure whether an offer includes returning customers. Use the troubleshooting checklist when a code is rejected or savings do not appear.
