Temu promo code for a first order: what qualifies?

Understand the difference between a new account, first app installation and first purchase before counting on a welcome offer.

Quick answer: A first order does not automatically entitle you to a new-user discount. Offers vary by account, region and time, and the campaign terms displayed inside Temu control eligibility.

First order versus first app visit

A retailer may distinguish new customers, first-time app users and people who have never completed a purchase. An installation or reinstallation alone does not establish eligibility. If the campaign excludes prior accounts or orders, making a new installation does not change those rules.

How to evaluate the actual welcome offer

  1. Sign in using the account you intend to purchase from and set the correct delivery country.
  2. Search the listed code in the app if that is the code's activation method; otherwise follow the stated coupon-field instructions.
  3. Read minimum spend, selected-item restrictions, discount caps, expiry and any first-purchase requirement.
  4. Check whether the advertised bundle is multiple vouchers rather than a one-order discount.
  5. Review the checkout total including delivery and applicable taxes before committing.

Compare real savings, not headline values

As a hypothetical calculation, 30% of $40 is $12 before caps. That is not a verified saving for any code on this page. A discounted price or voucher offer can be less useful than an item with a lower ordinary price elsewhere.

First-order FAQ

Does a new Temu account guarantee a first-order code?

No. Eligibility may depend on account history, device, campaign, country and the retailer's own rules. Review what the app shows.

Can I use a Temu first-order code after reinstalling?

Reinstalling the app is not proof of new-customer eligibility. Temu may evaluate past account activity.

Is a $100 coupon bundle the same as $100 off one order?

Not necessarily. A bundle may contain several coupons with separate minimum spends, expiry dates and conditions.

Where do I enter a Temu first-order code?

Some campaign codes are searched in the app, while others use a field at checkout. Follow the instructions associated with the specific campaign.
