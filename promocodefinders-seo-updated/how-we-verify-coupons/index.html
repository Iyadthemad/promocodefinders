How We Verify Coupons

This page defines our evidence standard and editorial process. The current Temu records have no independently documented successful redemption, so we do not label those codes verified.

What “verified” means

We reserve “verified” for an independently documented test showing a code applied to an eligible basket and reduced the payable total before payment. The record must identify the test date, region, account audience and relevant conditions. This establishes only the observed result in that context; it does not guarantee availability for another account or certify a completed purchase unless that is separately documented.

Our verification workflow

  1. Identify the exact code and inspect the retailer's campaign instructions and terms. A published code or affiliate link is not evidence of a working promotion.
  2. Record the test date, country or delivery region, account audience, device or entry route where relevant, eligible basket and any minimum spend, expiry, cap or exclusions.
  3. Check whether the code produces an offer and whether it actually changes the final payable total on the same basket. Distinguish a displayed campaign from an applied discount.
  4. Retain a dated record of the terms and result, with personal and payment details removed. Publish the scope of the result and an evidence summary before applying a verified label.
  5. Record failures and corrections in context, preserve earlier dated observations, and update the relevant hub, code page and tracker together.

Status labels and missing evidence

  • Not independently verified: there is no sufficient independent test record. It does not mean the code definitely works or definitely fails.
  • Verified in a stated context: the documented test meets the standard above, with its date and limitations visible.
  • Not available in a tested context: the recorded check did not produce the stated offer for that account, region or basket. This does not establish universal expiry.

Unknown audience, region, dates and restrictions stay marked as unknown or not independently checked. We do not infer new-user, reinstalled-user, percentage-discount, gift or bundle eligibility from a code's existence.

Update cadence and dates

Reviews are manual and evidence-led. We aim to review listed codes monthly and sooner when credible changed terms, new test evidence or a correction is reported. This is an editorial target, not a claim that monthly redemption testing has occurred. We do not refresh dates automatically. If no new test exists, the last test field stays unchanged or says no independent test recorded.

A content review date records a substantive editorial review. A last-checked date records an actual independent coupon test. Page modification dates and sitemap dates describe content changes, not proof of a working promotion. The tracker separates these events.

Why your result can differ

Promotions may depend on delivery country, currency, account history, campaign timing, device, entry route and basket contents. Installing or reinstalling an app does not establish new-user eligibility. Use the account eligibility guide and confirm the terms and total displayed to your own account.

Affiliate disclosure and editorial independence

Some retailer links are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. Compensation does not establish verification or justify a discount claim. Our affiliate links retain sponsored and nofollow attributes. Read the full affiliate disclosure; PromoCodeFinder is independent of Temu.

Report a correction

Send details through our contact page, including the code, observation date, region and relevant terms. Do not send account credentials or payment data. Reader reports are assessed as reports and do not by themselves qualify as independently verified tests.
