How PromoCodeFinder verifies coupon codes

Our goal is to separate a real recorded check from a copied promotional claim. We record when a code is manually checked, describe what was observed, and keep account-specific limitations visible.

Our verification standard

A code may be labeled Checked by publisher only after the publisher manually reviews it in the retailer's current app or offer flow and records the check date. This label does not mean every visitor will receive the same promotion.

What we record

  • The exact coupon or promo code.
  • The date of the manual check.
  • The user type the offer appears intended for, when that is clear.
  • The promotion shown or described during the check.
  • Important limits such as account, region, device, basket or campaign restrictions.

What we do not claim

We do not treat a changing date by itself as proof that a coupon works. We do not claim universal eligibility when an offer is account-specific, and we do not present an advertised bundle value as guaranteed savings on one order unless the underlying terms support that interpretation.

Why results can differ

Retailer promotions can vary by country, account history, device, new-user status, reinstall status, basket contents and active campaign. A code that is checked successfully by the publisher can therefore produce a different result for another user.

How verification dates are updated

PromoCodeFinder stores coupon status in one verification data file. After a real check, the publisher confirms the check in the website's GitHub workflow. The workflow records the current date and updates the tracker data. This prevents the site from displaying a fresh date when no check was actually performed.

Affiliate transparency

Some retailer links are affiliate links and may earn PromoCodeFinder a commission at no extra cost to the visitor. Affiliate relationships do not change the verification label or the date recorded for a coupon. See the affiliate disclosure for more information.

Verification rule

A fresh date should represent a fresh check. If the check is not recorded, the date should not move.
