Shipping time and fee: the checkout is the source

We cannot substantiate one guaranteed number of delivery days or one universal shipping fee for this country. Temu may show different estimates for local-warehouse items, international sellers and individual delivery addresses. Before paying, record the estimate on each item and the final order shipping charge. Do not treat a promotional shipping banner as proof every seller and basket qualifies.

Returns and refund conditions

Temu frequently advertises returns for many eligible products within a window of up to 90 days, but exclusions exist and some electronics have shorter policies. Inspect the product return badge, the order return window, packaging requirements and any charge for an additional return from the same order. Do not assume 90 days is universal.

Available payment methods

Temu may present card, wallet, local payment, or installment options according to the market and account. We cannot verify an exhaustive current list for every shopper; use the payment buttons actually shown at checkout and do not assume a method listed in another region will appear for you.

How to redeem a Temu code by region

  1. Set your real delivery country and ZIP or postal code on the Temu account you will use.
  2. Search the code in the Temu app if it is an app-search campaign; otherwise use the stated checkout coupon field.
  3. Confirm the offer applies to your country, account and selected items.
  4. Read the discount cap, eligible products and minimum spend, then compare shipping, taxes and final total.
  5. Only order when the final payable price is acceptable without relying on unearned future vouchers.
